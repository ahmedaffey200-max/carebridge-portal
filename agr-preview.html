3.0Purpose of AgreementUjeedada Heshiiska

This Agreement sets out the rights, responsibilities, and expectations of both parties. The Patient acknowledges and agrees that:

  • Carebridge International Inc. provides international healthcare coordination services only.
  • Carebridge connects patients with independent hospitals, physicians, and healthcare providers.
  • Carebridge is not a hospital, clinic, physician, or medical provider.
  • Carebridge does not diagnose illnesses, prescribe medication, or provide medical treatment.
  • All medical decisions are made solely by licensed healthcare professionals.

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a clear and fair understanding between Carebridge and the Patient regarding the coordination services to be provided.

Heshiiskan wuxuu qeexayaa xuquuqda, mas'uuliyaadka, iyo faa'iidooyinka labada dhinac. Bukaanku wuxuu aqoonsadaa oo ku waafaqsanyahay in:

  • Carebridge International Inc. bixisaa adeegyada iskaashiga daryeelka caafimaadka caalamiga ah oo keliya.
  • Carebridge waxay xidho bukaannada isbitaallada, dhakhaatiirta, iyo bixiyeyaasha daryeelka caafimaadka ee madaxbannaan.
  • Carebridge ma aha isbitaal, kilinik, dhakhtar, ama bixiye daryeel caafimaad.
  • Carebridge kuma ogaysiiso xanuunnada, kuma qorto daawada, kana ma bixiso daaweynta caafimaadka.
  • Dhammaan go'aannada caafimaadka waxaa sameeya xirfadlayaasha caafimaadka ee shatiyaysan oo keliya.

Ujeedada heshiiskan waa in la aasaaso faham cad oo xaq ah oo u dhexeeya Carebridge iyo Bukaanka ee la xiriira adeegyada iskaashiga la siin doono.

5.0Carebridge ServicesAdeegyada Carebridge

  • Medical case coordination and document handling.
  • Submission of medical records to hospitals and specialists.
  • Coordination of medical opinions and treatment proposals.
  • Hospital and physician referral assistance.
  • Appointment scheduling and coordination.
  • Treatment planning coordination between the Patient and providers.
  • Medical travel coordination, including flights and itinerary planning support.
