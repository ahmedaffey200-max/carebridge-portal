3.0Purpose of Agreement

This Agreement sets out the rights, responsibilities, and expectations of both parties. The Patient acknowledges and agrees that:

  • Carebridge International Inc. provides international healthcare coordination services only.
  • Carebridge connects patients with independent hospitals, physicians, and healthcare providers.
  • Carebridge is not a hospital, clinic, physician, or medical provider.
  • Carebridge does not diagnose illnesses, prescribe medication, or provide medical treatment.
  • All medical decisions are made solely by licensed healthcare professionals.

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a clear and fair understanding between Carebridge and the Patient regarding the coordination services to be provided.

4.0Scope of Services

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Carebridge may coordinate the following:

  • Medical case reviews and medical opinions.
  • Hospital and physician referrals.
  • Appointment scheduling and treatment planning.
  • Medical travel arrangements and visa support.
  • Accommodation coordination.
  • Airport assistance and local transportation coordination.
  • Translation assistance.
  • Follow-up coordination after treatment.

Any services outside the scope of this Agreement require the prior written approval of both parties.

5.0Carebridge Services

Carebridge International Inc. provides international healthcare coordination services, which may include:

  • Medical case coordination and document handling.
  • Submission of medical records to hospitals and specialists.
  • Coordination of medical opinions and treatment proposals.
  • Hospital and physician referral assistance.
  • Appointment scheduling and coordination.
  • Treatment planning coordination between the Patient and providers.
  • Medical travel coordination, including flights and itinerary planning support.
  • Medical visa guidance and supporting documentation.
  • Accommodation coordination (hotels, apartments, or hospital stays where available).
  • Airport pickup and drop-off coordination.
  • Local transportation coordination.
  • Translation and interpretation support.
  • Communication support between the Patient and healthcare providers.
  • Post-treatment follow-up coordination.

The Patient acknowledges that Carebridge is a coordination service provider only, does not provide medical treatment or medical advice, and that all medical care is provided exclusively by independent licensed healthcare providers.

6.0Carebridge Responsibilities

Carebridge agrees to:

  • Provide coordination services in a professional and timely manner.
  • Act in good faith at all times.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of Patient information.
  • Protect personal and medical data.
  • Communicate relevant updates to the Patient.
  • Coordinate with healthcare providers responsibly.
  • Provide reasonable support throughout the medical travel process.
  • Respect Patient dignity and privacy.
  • Maintain professional standards of service.

The obligations of Carebridge under this Agreement are limited to coordination services only.

7.0Services Not Provided

Carebridge does not provide any of the following:

  • Medical diagnosis.
  • Medical treatment or surgery.
  • Prescription of medication.
  • Medical advice or clinical recommendations.
  • Emergency medical or ambulance services.
  • Nursing or hospital care.
  • Legal advice.
  • Immigration decisions or visa approvals.
  • Airline or hotel operations.
  • Financial guarantees of any kind.

Carebridge also does not guarantee medical outcomes or recovery, treatment success or cure, hospital acceptance or doctor availability, appointment availability, visa approval, travel schedules or airline performance, or pricing stability from third-party providers.

8.0Medical Responsibility Disclaimer

  • All medical decisions are made solely by licensed healthcare professionals.
  • Carebridge staff are not medical professionals.
  • Carebridge does not replace medical advice or diagnosis.
  • The Patient must rely only on qualified doctors and hospitals for treatment decisions.
  • Any information provided by Carebridge is administrative or coordination-based only.

9.0No Guarantee of Medical Outcomes

Carebridge makes no guarantee regarding cure or recovery, treatment success, surgical outcomes, medical complications, hospital acceptance, doctor availability, treatment timelines, final medical results, visa approvals, or travel disruptions and delays.

The Patient acknowledges that all medical and travel outcomes involve inherent risks that are outside the control of Carebridge.

10.0Patient Cooperation

Effective coordination depends on the cooperation of the Patient. The Patient agrees to:

  • Provide accurate and complete information.
  • Submit required medical documents promptly.
  • Respond to communication in a timely manner.
  • Follow instructions from licensed healthcare providers.
  • Notify Carebridge of any changes in condition or travel plans.
  • Attend scheduled appointments where possible.

Delay or failure to cooperate may affect coordination outcomes.

11.0Patient Responsibilities

The Patient agrees to:

  • Provide accurate, complete, and truthful medical information.
  • Disclose full medical history, including prior treatments and conditions.
  • Provide valid passport and travel information.
  • Inform Carebridge of any changes in medical condition or contact details.
  • Follow all instructions provided by licensed healthcare providers.
  • Comply with hospital rules and policies.
  • Comply with immigration and travel laws of destination countries.
  • Make independent and informed medical decisions.
  • Communicate respectfully with Carebridge and third-party providers.
  • Pay all agreed service fees and third-party costs on time.

Inaccurate or incomplete information may affect medical coordination and outcomes.

12.0Medical Documentation Requirements

The Patient may be required to provide:

  • Medical reports and diagnosis records.
  • Laboratory test results.
  • Imaging results (MRI, CT scans, X-rays, and similar).
  • Previous treatment records and prescriptions.
  • Specialist reports.
  • Passport copy and travel documents.
  • Insurance documents, if applicable.
  • Any additional documents requested by hospitals or physicians.

Carebridge may require additional documentation for proper coordination.

13.0Authorization to Share Medical Information

  • The Patient authorizes Carebridge to collect, use, and share medical information.
  • Medical records may be shared with hospitals, physicians, clinics, and laboratories for evaluation purposes.
  • Information will be shared only for healthcare coordination purposes.
  • Carebridge will handle all information in accordance with its confidentiality obligations.
  • The Patient consents to the international transfer of medical data where required for treatment coordination.

14.0Hospital & Physician Selection

  • Carebridge may recommend hospitals and physicians based on medical condition, availability, and Patient preferences.
  • Recommendations are based on third-party information and provider availability.
  • The final selection of hospital and physician is always the Patient’s decision.
  • Carebridge does not control or guarantee the availability of any provider.

15.0Treatment Costs & Financial Responsibility

  • All treatment costs are determined by independent hospitals and healthcare providers.
  • Estimates may change based on medical findings or complications.
  • Additional procedures may result in additional costs.
  • The Patient is fully responsible for all medical, travel, accommodation, and related expenses.
  • Carebridge is not responsible for pricing changes by third parties.
  • All payments to hospitals or providers are separate from Carebridge service fees unless otherwise agreed.

17.0Travel & Visa Coordination

Carebridge may assist with medical visa guidance and documentation support, hospital invitation letter coordination (where available), travel itinerary planning assistance, and general travel coordination support. The Patient acknowledges that:

  • Visa approval is determined solely by government and immigration authorities.
  • Carebridge does not guarantee visa approval or entry into any country.
  • Carebridge is not responsible for visa refusals, delays, or immigration decisions.
  • Travel requirements may change based on government policies.

18.0Accommodation Coordination

Carebridge may coordinate hotel recommendations near hospitals, apartment or long-term stay options, family accommodation suggestions, and hospital-provided accommodation (where available). The Patient acknowledges that:

  • Accommodation agreements are strictly between the Patient and the provider.
  • Carebridge does not own, manage, or operate accommodation services.
  • Carebridge is not responsible for the pricing, availability, or quality of accommodation providers.

19.0Transportation Coordination

Carebridge may coordinate airport pickup and drop-off, local transportation, hospital transfers, and interpreter transportation support (where applicable). The Patient acknowledges that:

  • Carebridge is not a transportation company.
  • All transportation services are provided by independent third-party providers.
  • Carebridge is not responsible for delays, accidents, or service failures by transport providers.

20.0Translation & Interpretation Services

Carebridge may provide or coordinate language interpretation during medical consultations, translation of medical documents, and communication support between the Patient and healthcare providers. The Patient acknowledges that:

  • Translation services are for communication assistance only.
  • They do not replace medical advice from licensed professionals.
  • Carebridge is not responsible for interpretation errors by third parties.

21.0Communication Policy

Carebridge communicates with the Patient by email, WhatsApp, phone calls, video calls, and messaging platforms. The Patient acknowledges that:

  • Carebridge will use reasonable efforts to respond in a timely manner.
  • Communication may depend on time zones and provider availability.
  • The Patient must ensure contact details are accurate and up to date.
  • Important updates will be communicated as necessary for coordination.

22.0Privacy & Confidentiality

Carebridge International Inc. will treat all Patient information as strictly confidential. Confidential information includes medical records, personal data, travel details, financial information, and identification documents. Carebridge will:

  • Use confidential information only for healthcare coordination purposes.
  • Implement reasonable safeguards to protect data.
  • Not disclose confidential information except where required by law or necessary for medical coordination.

These confidentiality obligations extend to employees, contractors, and third-party partners involved in coordination.

23.0Medical Information Handling

Carebridge may collect medical records from the Patient, store medical information securely for coordination purposes, share relevant medical data with hospitals, physicians, and healthcare providers, and request additional medical information when required. Medical data is shared only for treatment coordination, is not used for unrelated purposes, and the Patient consents to such handling as part of this Agreement.

24.0International Transfer of Medical Data

  • Patient medical data may be transferred internationally for treatment coordination.
  • Hospitals and providers may be located in different countries.
  • The Patient consents to the cross-border transfer of medical records.
  • Carebridge will take reasonable steps to ensure the secure transmission of data.

25.0Electronic Communication & Consent

The Patient agrees to receive communications via email, WhatsApp, SMS, and phone, and to allow the electronic sharing of medical and travel information. Electronic communication is valid for coordination purposes. Carebridge is not responsible for delays or failures in electronic communication systems, and the Patient must ensure access to the communication channels provided.

26.0Electronic Signatures

  • Scanned copies, PDFs, and digital signatures are valid forms of agreement.
  • This Agreement may be executed electronically without physical signatures.
  • Electronic execution does not affect enforceability.

27.0Data Retention

Carebridge may retain Patient records for a reasonable period for legal, administrative, and coordination purposes. Retention may be required by law or regulatory obligations. The Patient may request information regarding data retention policies, subject to legal limitations.

28.0Compliance with Privacy Laws

Carebridge will comply with the applicable privacy and data protection laws relevant to its operations, including international data handling standards where applicable.

30.0Limitation of Medical Responsibility

  • Carebridge International Inc. does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, surgery, or prescriptions.
  • All medical services are provided exclusively by independent licensed healthcare providers.
  • Carebridge is not responsible for medical decisions made by doctors or hospitals.
  • The Patient acknowledges that all medical risks are inherent to treatment and remain between the Patient and the healthcare provider.

31.0Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Carebridge shall not be liable for any of the following:

  • Medical injury, complications, disability, or death.
  • Treatment failure or poor medical outcomes.
  • Hospital or physician negligence or malpractice.
  • Visa refusals, immigration delays, or entry denial.
  • Flight delays, cancellations, or airline issues.
  • Accommodation issues or third-party failures.
  • Transportation accidents or delays.
  • Loss of documents, baggage, or personal belongings.
  • Government actions, policy changes, or border closures.
  • Natural disasters, pandemics, or force majeure events.
  • Carebridge is not responsible for any financial losses related to third-party services.

Total liability. In all cases, the total liability of Carebridge under this Agreement, if any, is limited to the amount of service fees actually paid by the Patient to Carebridge.

32.0No Guarantee of Outcomes

Carebridge does not guarantee any medical, travel, or service outcomes, including treatment results, recovery, approvals, or timelines. All outcomes depend on independent third parties and individual circumstances.

33.0Independent Third-Party Providers

  • Hospitals, doctors, clinics, laboratories, airlines, hotels, transport providers, and translators are independent third parties.
  • They are not employees, agents, partners, or representatives of Carebridge.
  • Carebridge is not responsible for the acts, omissions, negligence, or performance of third parties.
  • Any contract for services is strictly between the Patient and the third-party provider.

34.0Force Majeure

Carebridge shall not be liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including:

  • War, terrorism, or civil unrest.
  • Natural disasters such as earthquakes, floods, fires, and storms.
  • Pandemics, epidemics, or public health emergencies.
  • Government actions, travel bans, or border closures.
  • Airline strikes or transportation disruptions.
  • Hospital closures or medical emergencies.
  • Power, communication, or system failures.
  • Any other event beyond the reasonable control of Carebridge.

35.0Insurance Responsibility

  • The Patient is solely responsible for obtaining appropriate travel, medical, and emergency insurance.
  • Carebridge does not provide insurance coverage.
  • Carebridge is not responsible for uninsured medical or travel costs.

Patients are strongly encouraged to obtain comprehensive international medical insurance before travelling.

36.0Patient Conduct & Professional Behaviour

The Patient agrees to behave respectfully and professionally at all times when interacting with Carebridge, hospitals, physicians, translators, transport providers, and any third parties, and agrees not to:

  • Harass, threaten, or abuse any staff or representatives.
  • Use offensive, discriminatory, or inappropriate language.
  • Disrupt coordination services or medical processes.
  • Request services outside the agreed scope without approval.
  • Engage in fraudulent or deceptive conduct.
  • Damage relationships between Carebridge and healthcare providers.

Carebridge may suspend or terminate services immediately if misconduct occurs.

37.0False Information, Fraud & Misrepresentation

The Patient agrees to provide truthful, complete, and accurate information at all times, disclose all relevant medical history and conditions, avoid submitting false or altered documents, and not misrepresent Carebridge services or staff. Carebridge is not responsible for consequences resulting from false, incomplete, or misleading information provided by the Patient.

38.0Non-Interference With Other Patients

  • The Patient must not interfere with or influence other Carebridge patients in a misleading or harmful way.
  • The Patient must not provide false, misleading, or unauthorized medical or travel advice to other patients.
  • The Patient must not impersonate Carebridge staff or representatives.
  • The Patient must not disrupt or interfere with other patients’ treatment coordination or decisions.
  • The Patient must respect the confidentiality and independence of all other patients.

This clause does not restrict truthful communication or lawful personal opinions.

39.0Non-Circumvention

  • The Patient agrees not to bypass Carebridge by directly engaging hospitals, doctors, clinics, or service providers introduced or coordinated by Carebridge for the purpose of avoiding service fees.
  • If the Patient independently engages such providers without Carebridge involvement, Carebridge shall not be responsible for coordination, support, outcomes, or services.
  • Carebridge retains the right to charge applicable fees if its introduced providers are used in connection with services originally coordinated by Carebridge.

40.0Suspension & Termination of Services

Carebridge may suspend or terminate services immediately if:

  • The Patient breaches this Agreement.
  • The Patient provides false or misleading information.
  • The Patient engages in abusive, threatening, or fraudulent conduct.
  • The Patient fails to make required payments.
  • The Patient violates hospital or legal requirements.
  • Continued service becomes unsafe or impractical.

Termination does not affect any rights accrued prior to termination.

41.0Intellectual Property & Confidential Information

  • All Carebridge systems, processes, documentation, pricing structures, templates, and operational methods are confidential and proprietary.
  • The Patient must not copy, reproduce, or distribute Carebridge materials without written permission.
  • The Patient must not share internal coordination processes or business methods with third parties.

This clause does not restrict the lawful personal use of documents received for medical purposes.
